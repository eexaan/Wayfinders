Why not move a bus instead?

Because the riders already on that route would be stranded. Moving an R1 trip onto R2 helps R2 and leaves R1's passengers at a stop with no bus — which is what the donor load after column in scenario_moves.csv already watches for. Proposals here are additive: a new trip, a new stop, or an extended line.
